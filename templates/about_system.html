Market2Farm – Centralized Agricultural Commerce

Market2Farm is an AI‑powered platform that connects farmers directly to buyers through a secure, admin‑mediated marketplace. All communication, payments, and logistics are coordinated by platform administrators to ensure trust and safety.

Key Features

  • AI Crop Disease Detection – Uses OpenAI to diagnose plant diseases from images and symptom descriptions.
  • Climate Intelligence – Real‑time weather forecasts with actionable farming advice.
  • Admin‑Centric Market – Products are approved by admins; buyer‑seller connections are mediated.
  • Secure Payments – M‑Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfer, PayPal, Stripe, and Binance Pay.
  • Inquiry System – Buyers can ask questions to admins; admins reply in a chat interface.
  • Notifications – In‑platform, email, and SMS alerts for orders, inquiries, and admin messages.
  • User Dashboard – View orders, wallet, cart, and manage profile.

Built with Flask, Tailwind CSS, and OpenAI. All data is encrypted and access is role‑based.
